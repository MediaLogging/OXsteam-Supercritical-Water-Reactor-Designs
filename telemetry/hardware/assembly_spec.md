# OXsteam SCWR Chamber & Manifold Hardware Specification

This document maps out the assembly pipeline configuration for manufacturing the physical 4-valve, 3-chamber block-and-bleed assembly assets.

## 🔩 1. Flange & Piping Structural Requirements
*   **Pressure Rating**: ANSI Class 2500 / API 10,000 (Designed to maintain stability at $30 \text{ MPa}$ operational limits).
*   **Connection Interface**: Ring Type Joint (RTJ) Flanges utilizing Oval Soft Iron gaskets to counter low-cycle thermal stress variations.
*   **Material Matrix**: 
    *   **Chamber Walls**: Inconel 625 (Required to resist dense supercritical water halogen-induced stress corrosion cracking).
    *   **Main Valve Trim**: Stellite 6 Coated Cobalt Alloy faces (Zero-clearance through-conduit sliding gates).
